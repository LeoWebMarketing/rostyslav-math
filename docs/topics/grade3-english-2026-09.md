## Section `can-activities` - "Я вмію! (can / can't)"
Things: shells - мушлі (sheet says «ракушки», use the correct «мушлі»), puzzles - пазли,
teddy bears - плюшеві ведмедики.
Activities: do karate - займатися карате, play basketball - грати в баскетбол, sing - співати,
dance - танцювати, paint - малювати (фарбами), dive - пірнати.
Grammar: can - могти, вміти; can't - не могти, не вміти; Can you ...? - Чи вмієш ти ...?;
Yes, I can. - Так, вмію. / No, I can't. - Ні, не вмію. Also he/she can: He can sing. She can't dive.
Lessons: 1) Things (choice, match, type); 2) Activities (choice with emoji pictures, match, speak);
3) can / can't sentences (order word chips, fill "can/can't", choice of the right answer to "Can you dance?").

## Section `dates` - "Як записати дату"
Days: Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday (+ Ukrainian, capital letter rule).
Ordinals 1-31: the first, second, third, fourth, fifth, sixth, seventh, eighth, ninth, tenth, eleventh,
twelfth, thirteenth ... twentieth, twenty-first ... thirtieth, thirty-first (tricky spellings: first,
second, third, fifth, eighth, ninth, twelfth, twentieth, thirtieth).
Months: of January ... of December.
Date format in the notebook: "Tuesday, the 15th of September" (day, comma, the + ordinal + of + month).
Extra: Classwork - класна робота, Homework - домашня робота, Ex. - вправа, P. - сторінка.
Lessons: 1) Days of the week (order days, match, what comes after Monday); 2) Months (order, match,
type); 3) Ordinals (match 3 - the third, type "the twelfth", choose correct spelling); 4) Write the
date (order chips "Tuesday, / the / 15th / of / September", fill the gap, choose the correct date line
for a given date, Classwork/Homework match).

## Section `numbers-10-100` - "Числа 10-100"
Teens: ten, eleven, twelve, thirteen, fourteen, fifteen, sixteen, seventeen, eighteen, nineteen, twenty.
Tens: twenty, thirty, forty (no "u"!), fifty, sixty, seventy, eighty, ninety, one hundred.
Also compound: twenty-one ... ninety-nine with hyphen.
Lessons: 1) 10-20 (match digit-word, type the word, listen and choose); 2) Tens (match, spot the
mistake: fourty vs forty, choose); 3) Mixed 21-99 (type "thirty-five", choose the number for
"sixty-eight", order chips, thirteen vs thirty listening pairs).

Rules for authors: every English word/sentence has `speak: true`; Ukrainian must be correct literary
Ukrainian; each lesson 8-12 exercises mixing at least 3 types; accept both "can't" and "cannot" where
natural; apostrophe variants handled by the normalizer.
