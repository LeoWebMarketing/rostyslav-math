## Have got - grammar table (classwork 8 September) + more examples (photos 6-7)
Positive: I/You/We/They have got = 've got; He/She/It has got = 's got. (Я, ти, ми, вони, він, вона, воно)
Negative: I/you/we/they have not got = haven't got; he/she/it has not got = hasn't got. (не має)
Question: Have I/you/we/they got ...? Has he/she/it got ...? (Чи маєш...?)
Short answers: Yes, I/you/we/they have. Yes, he/she/it has. No, I/you/we/they have not / haven't.
No, he/she/it has not / hasn't.
Examples: Він має собаку. = He has got a dog. Вона має кота. = She has got a cat. She hasn't got a cat.
Has she got a cat? Yes, she has. No, she hasn't. He has got a basketball. He has not got a basketball.
Can with we: Ми вміємо малювати. = We can paint. We can't paint. Can we paint? Yes, we can. No, we can't.
